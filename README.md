# Hush

*Make Chromium a little calmer, and a little more magical.*

Hush turns every new tab into a quiet, beautiful place that feels like your own. A softer landing for busy days, with room for the little things you want close — and a change of scenery whenever you need one.

Everything is gentle by design: soft light, unhurried motion, and just enough space to breathe. Open a tab, settle in, and let the noise fade.

## Install

1. Download the ZIP and unzip it.
2. In Chromium, open the extensions page and turn on Developer mode.
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the folder you just unzipped.
4. Open a new tab. That's it.
